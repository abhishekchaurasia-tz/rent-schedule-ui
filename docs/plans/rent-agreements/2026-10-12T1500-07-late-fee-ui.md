# Late fee UI: read, apply and remove an invoice's late fee

Billing applies late fees since its spec `17-late-fee-consumer.md` (v13), and has routes to read, apply and
remove one. This UI has nothing for them yet: the invoice list does not show a late fee, no screen calls the
three routes, and Update Invoice labels a late fee line by its wire name, `LateFeeCharge`, because the
catalog it labels lines from never lists the system "Late Fee" row.

```
/invoices row ──⋮ Late fee…──► LateFeePanel ──GET …/late-fee──► figures, marks, lines
                                   │ Remove late fee ──DELETE──► re-read until the invoice catches up
                                   │ Apply a list    ──PUT─────► applied / skipped, re-read
Update Invoice ── LateFeeCharge line reads "Late Fee" + a notice of what editing does
```

### Milestone 3 — Remove, and the re-read (FR 5 to FR 7, FR 12, FR 13)

- [x] **Remove late fee**, only while the status is not `voided` or `deleted`, with the inline confirmation of
  FR 6.
- [x] The re-read of FR 12: after a `204` (and in M4 after an applied list), `get` again every second, at most
  ten times, until `lateFeeAmount` reads what the change leaves: the paid part read before a removal, the
  list's total after an applied list. A removal with nothing unpaid does not wait. Use RxJS `timer` and
  `takeWhile`, unsubscribed when the panel closes. *(Corrected before coding: "until the amount or line count
  differs" would poll ten times after a removal with nothing to remove, and stop too early on the marks, which
  change before the figures.)*
- [x] Specs (`fakeAsync`): `DELETE` called once on confirm; the stop when the amount reads the paid part;
  no wait when nothing was unpaid; **Refresh** after ten reads that never got there; no read after close;
  `409` and both `422`s rendering their `detail`.

- [x] Specs: one per FR 10 rule, with no request sent; the body sent, the amount worked out; an empty list

### As built

- **The re-read after an applied list runs only when a line changed.** M4 says an applied answer starts the
  re-read. An applied list that changed no line leaves the late fee as it was, so there is nothing to wait
  for; FR 12, as corrected, says the same.
- **The Update Invoice notice reads the invoice as loaded, not the form.** M5 says "shown only when a line is
  `LateFeeCharge`". A late fee line removed in the form still stops further late fees once saved (backend
  BR-19), so the notice stays until the page is reloaded.
- **Saving a late fee line needed no code.** Update Invoice already sends each line's `lineId` and
  `lineItemId` as it was seeded; the new spec proves it for a late fee line, after another line is edited,
  since a save with nothing changed sends nothing.
- **The matrix also corrects §5.** It listed a late fee double-charge guard as not built in the backend; it
  is, through the keyed lines and `older_calculation`, and the bullet now says so.
- **No existing spec was edited.** The suite went from 556 to 591.

## 3. Scope & Context Rules

**May be modified:** the files the milestones name, `src/styles.scss` only to share a style the panel would
otherwise copy, and `docs/ui-automation-test-matrix.md`.

**Must not be touched:** the backend (`innago-rent-accounting`); the scope interceptor; any other screen.

- No new package.
- No `localStorage` or `sessionStorage`.
- Where an existing spec has to change, stop and report it rather than editing it to pass.

### Technical Decisions

| # | Decision | Chosen | Rejected, and why | Source |
|---|----------|--------|-------------------|--------|
| 1 | Feature folder | `rent-agreements`, as `03` (Update Invoice) and `04` (the invoice list) | A new `late-fees` folder: every UI spec lives in the one folder, and these two screens are where the late fee is shown | Mine, following the repository's layout |
| 2 | Where the late fee actions live | The list's ⋮ menu, opening a side panel | An invoice detail page: none exists, and the list is where Delete and Void already live (spec `04`) | Mine |
| 3 | Offer the apply form | Yes | No: this UI is the API's test harness, and `PUT …/late-fee` exists for testing (backend D28) | Mine, from the repository's standing rule |
| 4 | Calculation time input | ISO 8601 UTC text, starting as now | The Material datepicker: it holds a date, not an instant, and BR-04 compares instants | Mine |
| 5 | The amount | Worked out as quantity × rate | Typed: a typed amount that is not quantity × rate is what BR-38 refuses; to send one, use Postman's `Negative / 12` | Mine |
| 6 | Waiting for the invoice | A bounded re-read (1 s × 10) | A single re-read, which usually comes too soon; polling until closed, which never stops on a stuck reconcile | Mine |
| 7 | Texts | Backend spec 17's | Product copy: none is settled for this UI | The repository's standing rule |
